# LegacyKeeper — Art Direction

## Rejected directions

The Bridgefold was too abstract and logistical. The Bastion Seal felt like a
banking or vault product and its brass theme conflicted with KeeperHub. A plain
shield, padlock, or keyhole would correct the category but remain generic.

## Signature element — The Sentinel Gate

Two angular dark-green security jaws face inward around one bright signal-green
core. Together they create a closed defensive perimeter, but they never trace a
stock shield outline. A single narrow channel runs from the protected core to
the lower edge: the only way out is the verified KeeperHub execution path.

The core is a compact vertical signal with one precise offset notch—not an ECG
waveform, checkmark, letter, or power symbol. It represents the owner’s live
authorization state. The jaws represent continuous monitoring and fail-closed
policy enforcement. The controlled channel represents the sponsored onchain
transaction and its receipt trail.

This should read in order: **security perimeter → live monitored state → one
verified action path**. It is an active cyber-security mechanism, not a family,
memorial, logistics, or banking emblem.

## Material language

KeeperHub-adjacent command infrastructure: deep matte greens, hard planar cuts,
tight seams, and one crisp high-energy green signal. No brass, paper, friendly
softness, glass, chrome, glow cloud, or ornamental embossing.

## Composition

Compact and vertically anchored. The dark jaws carry most of the visual mass;
the bright core occupies less than one quarter of the mark. Negative space
between them must remain visible at 16px. The lower execution channel is short
and structural, never a dangling stem or exclamation mark.

## Palette

- Signal accent: KeeperHub green `#09FD67`
- Active green: `#06B171`
- Structural green: `#043D2B`
- Deep ground: `#031B13`
- Near-black green: `#020A07`

The bright signal green is the only high-saturation accent. All other colours
are dark or low-saturation greens.

## Forbidden list

- No text, initials, letters, numbers, wordmarks, or watermarks inside the icon.
- No plain shield, padlock, key, keyhole, vault wheel, bank door, or police badge.
- No brass, gold, cream-paper, heritage seal, family crest, tree, or tombstone.
- No eye, camera aperture, crosshair, radar sweep, fingerprint, or target reticle.
- No ECG zigzag, checkmark, power-button ring, exclamation mark, or dangling stem.
- No circuit board, crypto hexagon, network-node web, chain, robot, brain, or sparkle.
- No rainbow, second bright accent, purple, blue, gradient, bloom, glass, or chrome.
- No rounded app tile, soft blob, thin outline, clip art, or stacked metaphors.

## Dual-legibility target

At 16px, the mark reads as dark protective jaws around a bright secured core. At
512px, the offset notch, layered jaw construction, and controlled execution
channel reveal the product’s monitored-and-verified action model.
