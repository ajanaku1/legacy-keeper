# Sentinel Gate Candidate Critique

## A — Gate — Grade: B

- **Concept fit:** Two hardened access-control doors isolate the live core, clearly expressing fail-closed execution.
- **Brand compliance:** Exact KeeperHub-inspired greens, no gradient, text, crypto motif, shield, lock, or decorative effect.
- **Craft:** The nested door geometry is clean and disciplined, with enough colour separation to imply layered controls.
- **Legibility:** The core and opposing doors survive at 16px, although the silhouette can briefly resemble code brackets.
- **Ownability:** The gate can extend naturally into transaction states and workflow boundaries, but it is less immediately protective than C.

**Verdict:** Strong product logic and clean execution; its category ambiguity at first glance is a structural limitation.

## B — Lock — Grade: B

- **Concept fit:** The split shackle and monitored core provide the fastest literal reading of protected liveness.
- **Brand compliance:** Uses no keyhole, checkmark, text, gradient, or off-palette colour; the lock is transformed by the split authorization state.
- **Craft:** Bold, balanced, and highly legible, though intentionally more conventional than the other candidates.
- **Legibility:** Best candidate at 16px; the lock silhouette and signal core remain unmistakable.
- **Ownability:** The green core adds product relevance, but padlock geometry is crowded category territory.

**Verdict:** The safest immediate-security choice, with a structural distinctiveness ceiling caused by the familiar lock form.

## C — Shield — Grade: B

- **Concept fit:** Split defensive panels, a monitored core, and an open verified seam communicate protection plus controlled execution in one object.
- **Brand compliance:** The shield is not a stock solid glyph: two asymmetric systems create a secondary protected form in negative space, without a checkmark, keyhole, or crest decoration.
- **Craft:** Strong outer silhouette, deliberate central seam, and three green values create depth while remaining exact flat vector artwork.
- **Legibility:** The defensive outline and signal core remain clear at 16px; secondary interior geometry rewards larger sizes.
- **Ownability:** The split-panel construction and negative-space inner guard are the most defensible signature in the set.

**Verdict:** Best balance of immediate security recognition and a LegacyKeeper-specific monitored-execution story; remaining refinements are optical only.

## Product-user 10-second test

- **A:** B — feels like hardened access control, but takes a moment to resolve.
- **B:** A — instantly says security, though it resembles familiar password-product marks.
- **C:** A — instantly protective and more distinctive than a conventional shield.

## Final recommendation

1. **C — Shield** — strongest combination of security vibe, KeeperHub palette, and ownable construction.
2. **A — Gate** — most faithful to the fail-closed execution model.
3. **B — Lock** — clearest category signal, least distinctive identity.

C wins because the concept is finally legible before its explanation: it looks like security first, then reveals monitored state and controlled execution on closer inspection.
