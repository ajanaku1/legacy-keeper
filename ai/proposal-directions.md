# LegacyKeeper — Codex Proposal Directions

## A — Dead Man's Console

- DNA: `DNA-A-SECURITY-AMBER-ASYMMETRIC`
- Stance: industrial security console
- DFII: 13/15 — impact 5, fit 5, feasibility 4, performance 4, consistency risk 5
- Type: Syne for structural commands; Chivo Mono for evidence and telemetry
- Color: near-black, signal amber, recovery green, emergency coral
- Rhythm: 4px base; hard rails, one-pixel evidence dividers, near-zero radius
- Motion: only press, dialog and confirmation feedback; no ambient motion
- Anchor: the audit ledger sits between a dead-man circuit and a striped physical
  panic cover.
- Differentiation: it treats the product as a safety instrument, not a generic
  card dashboard.

## B — Continuity Plan

- DNA: `DNA-B-CALM-WARM-CONTINUITY`
- Stance: calm product minimalism
- DFII: 13/15 — impact 4, fit 5, feasibility 5, performance 5, consistency risk 6
- Type: Manrope for reassuring product copy; DM Mono for proof
- Color: parchment, deep forest, soft sage, emergency clay
- Rhythm: 4px base; broad breathing room and two restrained radii
- Motion: quiet confirmation reveals and direct press feedback
- Anchor: a single protection story flows from the promise, through people, to
  the dark evidence record.
- Differentiation: it frames inheritance as continuity and care instead of
  crypto operations.

## C — Forensic Trace

- DNA: `DNA-C-FORENSIC-DENSE-TRACE`
- Stance: forensic operator tooling
- DFII: 14/15 — impact 5, fit 5, feasibility 5, performance 4, consistency risk 5
- Type: Archivo for compressed hierarchy; Fragment Mono for trace data
- Color: blue-black, electric cyan, retry orange, failure coral
- Rhythm: 4px base; zero-radius cells and explicit grid lines
- Motion: instant filters, minimal control feedback, no table animation
- Anchor: failed and recovered attempts are visually bound as one trace chain.
- Differentiation: it makes retry lineage the product's central object instead
  of hiding it inside status cards.
